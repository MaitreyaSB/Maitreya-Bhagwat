About

Whatever holds still long enough.

Photographer based in Maharashtra. I picked up my uncle’s bridge camera at 14 and found my footing in black-and-white street and documentary work.

It has spread out since then — ritual, architecture, whatever holds still long enough. This is the personal work. On the commercial side I run Metal Eye Visual Studios for industrial and product photography.

Six years in, still shooting every week.
